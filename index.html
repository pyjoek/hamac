Who We Are

A space that holds
culture with both hands

HAMAC was born from a simple belief: that art belongs to everyone and culture is best explored together. We are an open collective of artists, curators, and storytellers bridging drawing, painting, and living cultural practice.

From intimate sketches to monumental works, every piece carries a conversation — about identity, place, memory, and what it means to make something by hand.
